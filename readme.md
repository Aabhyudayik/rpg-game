# JS RPG GAME

## How to play
1. Open `index.html` in your web browser.
2. Use the buttons on the screen to attack, defend, or select weapons.
3. Check your stats and the enemy’s stats to plan your moves.
4. Each weapon has different attack power — choose wisely!
5. Keep playing until you defeat all enemies.

## Weapons
1. Stick power: 5 
2. Dagger power: 30 
3. Claw hammer power: 50 
4.  Sword power: 100
5. Scythe power:200
